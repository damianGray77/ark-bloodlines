When Single Player Settings is checked, ARK’s hidden Health and Melee factors are applied on top without changing these base values. Breeding point provenance does not depend on them.

QUALITY SCORE

Species breeding goals

Choose whether each inherited stat should be high, low, or ignored. Grades are relative to the active dinos in that species line; player levels and imprint never inflate genetic quality.

Default targets: high Health, Stamina, Weight, and Melee; Oxygen, Food, and Speed are ignored. A low target is useful when you deliberately breed waste stats down. Grades use target-gene rank, progress over tracked parents, mutation capacity, and aligned Frail/Robust/Mutable traits.
